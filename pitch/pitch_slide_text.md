Title: Organoid growth charts: a centile chart for brain-organoid cell-type composition

Left side:
- Kids have growth charts. Brain organoids don't. We make one.
- Data: HNOCA, 322 samples, 4 protocols. x = days in culture, y = progenitor fraction (NPC + IP).
- Method: normative modelling (the family behind brain charts). PCNtoolkit, Bayesian linear regression with B-spline mean and age-dependent variance, publication as batch effect.

Right side: pitch_slide_centile_chart.png

Bottom:
- Deliverable: the centile chart + z-score for every organoid. Stretch: how many organoids are needed before the centiles stabilize (subsampling).
- Looking for: anyone who took the normative modelling tutorial, Python / pandas, or just curious. No organoid background needed.
